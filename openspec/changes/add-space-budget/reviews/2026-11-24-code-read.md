Fable, fresh context, read-only, after the code landed (`e046e65`, `761ef8a`): `reflect_live_cells`, `Reflect/Space.lean`, `Wire/Form.lean`, the Surface entry points, the exports and their Zig callers, the contract, and the three check scripts. **Verdict: SEND-BACK**, for one small round on findings 1 to 3. No memory-safety defect in the C walk, no mismatch between an export and its callers, no contract statement weaker than it reads.

## Defects

1. **The written-answer bound admits twice the items the design calls the worst case.** `writesGo` charged four bytes for a small number or an empty text, so `d.writes (4 * cells)` passed a tree of about `cells` items. The design counted items made, but the bound is on the tree, and sharing gives tree items for nothing: an array of 1000 zeros consed 3990 times onto an outer array holds about 20000 cells and counts 15.98 MB written. At the printer's measured rates four million short items are about 330 MiB against the image's 360.
2. **`fitsValue` charged a captured data value about 6 and `valueData` builds about 88 bytes for it** (`.arr [.nat 0, d]` and a list cell). A thousand closures over one environment of 2670 small values pass while holding about 5000 cells, and the form then built is about 235 MB, on the network path, before `need > max_payload` refuses it. Code was closer: 100 an instruction against 250 to 300 bytes built.
3. **`checkNat` was charged 2 and rebuilds three objects** (`Data.nat`, `MValue.data`, the list cell), against the design's own rule that a matched value built again is charged as new. Every other case of `owed` is at or above the objects `step` makes.

## Remarks

4. `valueData` and `valuesData` recurse on closure nesting, and `write` and `renderData` likely on array nesting: a chain of 150000 closures passes `fitsValue` and would fill the image's 16 MiB stack. Not introduced here (`homoiconic-q59.72`).
5. The stack comment in `ffi.c` said one entry for each cell counted; it is the fields but the first, up to four for an instruction of code.
6. For a space above about 2^62 the clamped answer of a failed count is below the bar. Unreachable: a ledger that size is never spent.
7. `slots * 3`, `slots * 4` and `room * 2 + fields` overflow only at sizes that cannot be allocated.
8. `runTurn`'s comment said a driver sees a state that is not halted either way, which is false when the turn began at a halted state; and a turn out of space is reported in the words for a turn out of steps.
9. The text bound allows four bytes a character; the renderer was not read for longer escapes.

Nothing wrong was found in: the walk's termination, its table's load across growth, its exits and its counts; the two shapes of `runSpace` against `charge`; `writesGo`'s termination and how far it looks; the tail call in `cellsItems`; the four exports' arguments against `host/run.zig`, `host/check.zig`, `unikernel/main.zig`, `unikernel/net.zig` and `unikernel/reply.zig`; the contract's statements against the design's; `trail` against `runSpace`; and the check scripts' controls.

## Answered

1. An item of an array is counted five and itself, not two: three more than it writes, for what the printer holds. Four bytes for each cell then admit at most 2.28 million items of any kind, about what a 2 MiB literal holds (2097145 empty texts are 14.7 million and still answer). The reader's program is a case in `kernel/Surface/Check.lean`, `host/check.sh` and `unikernel/check.sh`: held 3990 times it is refused, held 2000 times it is written.
2. A value in a function's form is counted forty and what it writes, and an instruction three hundred. A thousand functions over one environment of 3000 values are refused and a hundred are allowed (`kernel/WireReplyCheck.lean`).
3. `checkNat` is charged 3.
4. Added to `homoiconic-q59.72`.
5, 6, 8. The comments say what is so.
9. `Surface.renderText` writes a line end, a tab, a backslash and a double quote as two bytes and every other character as itself, at most four bytes of UTF-8.
