Consequently neither unreadable halted values nor guard-free replacement closures can escape the contract. Combined with `machine_correct` and source determinism, it pins the exact compiled form of the specific source outcome. This is stronger than decompilation equality and is not vacuous: `identity_halts` constructs a seven-step halt (`MachineExact.lean:39`).

It does not exclude reaching absorbing `fault`. The revised design (`design.md:25`) and `Machine.lean:84` explicitly acknowledge that unproved invariant. For a terminating compiled program, forward correctness and fault absorption exclude any earlier fault. The delta requires never halting, not a proof that every divergent compiled run stays in `running`, so the remaining limitation is not a contract gap.
